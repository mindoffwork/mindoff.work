About

Hi, I'm Joe, a product engineer and researcher. MindOff is where I share the things I design, build, and learn along the way.

I started this space to keep a record of my projects, discoveries, and the ways I approach making things. Much of my work is research and development: exploring an idea, building it out, and refining it into something useful and simple.

I share it in the open because ideas are more useful when others can learn from them and take them further. If something here helps you create something of your own, I am happy for you.
